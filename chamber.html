The Guardian
Behavioural risk monitoring — not financial advice. By default Iris only watches and cannot act on your account. If you have explicitly consented to the Guardian lock, she may close open positions and cancel pending orders on a connected account when you breach your limits. She cannot prevent an order being sent to your broker, and cannot guarantee any loss is prevented. You can withdraw consent at any time, with immediate effect.
